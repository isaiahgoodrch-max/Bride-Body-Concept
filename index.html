Questions

Before you book

My wedding is soon. Is it too late?
Almost never. Brides have seen real change in six weeks. The plan just looks different when the countdown is short, and we will be honest with you on the call about what is realistic.
What does coaching cost?
Packages are built around your timeline, so pricing depends on how long we work together. We cover it openly on your free call, with zero pressure to sign up on the spot.
Do I need a gym membership?
No. Your plan is built around what you actually have access to, whether that is a full gym, a home setup, or a hotel room while you travel for showers and bachelorette weekends.
Do I have to count every calorie?
You get custom macro targets, but the goal is a way of eating you can live with. Most brides are surprised by how much food that turns out to be.
Can my bridesmaids join me?
Yes. The Bride Tribe option lets you bring your bridal party or your mom along. Accountability gets a lot easier when someone else is doing it with you.
