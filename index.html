Questions

Before you book

What does coaching cost?
Packages are built around your timeline, so pricing depends on how long we work together. We cover it openly on your free call, and there is zero pressure to sign up on the spot.
My wedding is soon. Is it too late?
Almost never. Brides have seen real change in six weeks. The plan just looks different when the countdown is short, and we will be honest with you on the call about what is realistic.
Do I need a gym membership?
No. Plans are built around what you actually have access to, whether that is a full gym, a home setup, or a hotel room while you travel for showers and bachelorette weekends.
Do I have to count every calorie?
You will get custom macro targets, but the goal is a way of eating you can live with. Most brides are surprised by how much food that turns out to be.
Is this just about losing weight?
No. It is about how you look in your dress and how you feel in your body. For a lot of brides that means building strength and shape, not shrinking.
Can my bridesmaids join me?
Yes. The Bride Tribe option lets you bring your bridal party or your mom along. Accountability gets a lot easier when someone else is doing it with you.
What happens on the free call?
About twenty minutes. We talk goals, timeline, your dress, and what has not worked before. You leave with a clear gameplan either way.
